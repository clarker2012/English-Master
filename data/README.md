# Assessment word bank

`assessment-word-bank.json` contains 10,000 frequency-ranked English words with Chinese meanings, phonetics, part-of-speech labels, and rank metadata for the local objective vocabulary assessment.

Source: `chenny/english-word-10000`, derived from the Google 10,000 English frequency list with Chinese definitions and pronunciation metadata.

The original Google frequency-list license note is preserved in `google-10000-english-LICENSE.md`.
